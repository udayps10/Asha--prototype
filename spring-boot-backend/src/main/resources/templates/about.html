About Aasha

Technology in service of
human dignity.

Aasha (आशा — Hindi for "hope") is the national AI-powered disaster response platform for locating missing persons during natural calamities. Developed under the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) initiative to ensure rapid family reunification across India.

Process
01

Relief Officers Register Survivors

Officers at camps enter survivor details — name, age, photo, and medical status — into the Aasha platform via mobile or web.

02

AI Normalizes & Indexes Records

Names are phonetically normalized across 12 Indian languages. Photos are embedded into a vector database for facial similarity search.

03

Families Sign In and Search

Sign in to search by name, age, location or photo. Search and response records are available only after login.

04

AI Surfaces Best Matches

Our model ranks results by phonetic similarity, age proximity, and district overlap — returning matches even for misspelled names.

05

Families Reunite

Confirmed matches are communicated via SMS, WhatsApp, or in-app notification. Officers coordinate handover with the family.

Privacy & Safety

No Aadhaar stored

We use Aadhaar only for identity verification at entry. Numbers are never persisted in our database.

Photo encryption

All photos are AES-256 encrypted at rest. Facial embeddings are stored separately from personal details.

Critical record isolation

Deceased and critical records are in an isolated schema, access-logged, and require officer authentication.

Right to erasure

All person records are auto-purged 90 days after a disaster is officially declared over by NDMA.
